GAME MODES

Free modePLAYING

The open sandbox. Drive anywhere, set the wave height and top speed in Settings, drop anchor, switch cameras and trigger Wharfie Time.

DistancePLAYING

How far can you get without capsizing? You start moored at a beach jetty on a flat, calm sea, then head straight into the oncoming waves. Steer with A / D or ← / → — the boat runs at a fixed cruising speed and nothing else can be changed except the camera angle.

  • Credits: you earn 10 credits for every 100 m.
  • It gets harder: the sea starts flat and builds the further you go, mixing the old rigid swells with the new rolling seas and steep rogue waves.
  • Tidal waves: a giant wave rushes at you every 1,000 m — and each one is bigger and steeper than the last.
  • Capsizing: waves hitting you side-on, sharp turns at speed, and crash landings off big crests will flip the boat. One capsize ends the run.

Tips: keep the heading dot in the middle of the bar, ease the wheel instead of yanking it, and square up to a tidal wave before it arrives.
